Email Exposure Checker

See what is publicly linked to an email address: data breaches, Gravatar profile, GitHub accounts and mail domain info.

Use this to check your own email. The address is sent from your browser to third-party services (XposedOrNot, GitHub, Google DNS). Gravatar only receives a hash. This site stores nothing. Only public data is shown; do not use it to harass or track others.
Sources: XposedOrNot, Gravatar, GitHub public search, Google DNS. Results depend on those free services and may be incomplete.
